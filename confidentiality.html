Confidentiality

I understand the importance of professionals feeling safe and secure when consulting me as a professional's reputation is regarded as their most valuable asset. As a lawyer therapist who works independently and who is paid directly by you, without being funded by professional bodies where names, demographics and areas of treatment are retained, I assure you utmost privacy.

In addition, I will avoid scheduling consecutive appointments to further ensure your privacy. My office is located in a busy medical building in downtown Toronto which also provides anonymity. In some cases I will meet you in the community.
