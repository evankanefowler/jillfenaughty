Therapist  &  Lawyer
A Dual Lens

Consultation, coaching, counselling and psychotherapy is offered in a discreet office setting in downtown Toronto. I can offer assistance to legal professionals in the following areas:

  • Assistance in understanding current problems or barriers to success
  • Assistance in problem-solving and strategizing to enable the achievement of one's goals
  • Assistance in understanding issues from the past and in unearthing core values
  • Stress
  • Anxiety and depression
  • Substance abuse and dependence
  • Career/work issues (downsizing, advancement, transition, challenges of private practice, authenticity, do I really want law? What do I want my life to look like going forward? Redefining one's definition of success to be consistent with one's true values)
  • Self-esteem
  • Work place harassment
  • Eating disorders
  • Mindfulness
  • Couples Therapy

I will work with individuals, couples and families. Often the under functioning or over functioning of an individual will negatively impact a couple and the whole family.

I also offer consulting services for law firms.
